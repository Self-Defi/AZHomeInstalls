Our mission

Starting small, investing deeply

Two trainees.
Individual attention.

TradeStart is structured around two paid young adults rotating field assignments based on available work and opportunities to learn.

Who is TradeStart designed for?

High-school graduates not entering college or the military right away, including those who are undecided about their next chapter. Participants must be at least 18 years old.

What are we looking for?

Willingness to learn, reliability, respectful communication, and commitment to safe work. Previous trade experience is not a prerequisite.

Our standards

Meaningful work. Responsible supervision.

TradeStart participants complete only tasks appropriate to their training, supervision, safety requirements, and AHI’s authorized scope of work. Scheduling and paid hours vary with customer demand. TradeStart is a paid work-experience pathway, not an accredited school or registered apprenticeship. Expressing interest does not guarantee a position.

Interested in joining TradeStart?

High-school graduates can introduce themselves and learn about opportunities. Families, coaches, and workforce partners are welcome to connect.
