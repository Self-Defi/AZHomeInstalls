Not sure what's next?
Start by building something.

College isn't everyone's next step. Neither is the military. AHI TradeStart is a developing initiative for high-school graduates who want to earn a fair wage, gain real work experience, and explore skilled trades while deciding their future.

More than an internship

A paid bridge between high school and adulthood.

This isn't a conventional internship. It's a chance to take the next step with a paycheck, practical experience, real responsibility, and room to discover your direction.

TradeStart is being developed around two rotating, paid positions, with a planned starting wage of $20 per hour when hiring begins.

Our mission

Earn. Learn. Find your direction.

A first job can be much more than a paycheck. It can create confidence, teach accountability, and open a door to an unexpected career.

AHI TradeStart is being designed as a paid bridge from high school to real-world work. We want recent graduates to be valued team members—not unpaid interns or inexpensive labor.

Hands-on experience

What participants could gain

Actual assignments would be determined by job availability, safety requirements, and appropriate supervision.

01 • Fair pay from day one

$20/hour starting wage

We plan to pay a respectful starting wage for real work—not offer unpaid internships. Hiring has not yet started, and hours will depend on available assignments.

02 • Practical experience

Learn the fundamentals

Jobsite preparation, safe material handling, measuring, tools, teamwork, customer respect, and supervised residential installation tasks.

03 • Future options

Build confidence and direction

Documented workplace experience and exposure to trades, whether the next step is employment, further training, school, or entrepreneurship.

Starting small, investing deeply

Two trainees.
Individual attention.

Our initial concept is to employ two young adults and rotate field assignments based on available work and opportunities to learn.

Who is TradeStart designed for?

High-school graduates not entering college or the military right away, including those who are undecided about their next chapter. We plan to start with adults age 18 or older.

What are we looking for?

Willingness to learn, reliability, respectful communication, and commitment to safe work. Previous trade experience is not a prerequisite.

Transparency

Building the program responsibly.

AHI TradeStart is currently a planned initiative, not an active hiring program. Expressing interest does not constitute an application or guarantee employment. The program's launch, availability, planned $20/hour starting wage, and schedules depend on business demand, insurance, legal employment requirements, safety supervision, and authorized work scope. This is not an accredited training institution or registered apprenticeship.

Want to hear more?

Graduates, families, coaches, and workforce partners can reach us as we develop TradeStart.
