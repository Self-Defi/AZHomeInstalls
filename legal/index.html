Legal notice

Service limitations & handyman-exemption disclosure

Not a Licensed Contractor

AZHomeInstalls is not an Arizona licensed contractor. AZHomeInstalls limits its services to work that it determines may be performed without an Arizona contractor license under an applicable statutory exemption.

Operating limits

AZHomeInstalls will accept only work that is casual or minor in nature and that fits within the applicable Arizona exemption. For work relying on A.R.S. § 32-1121(A)(14), AZHomeInstalls applies an aggregate project-price limit of less than $1,000 under the statute’s calculation rules and will not perform work that requires a local building permit.

AZHomeInstalls will not divide a larger or major operation into smaller contracts to avoid contractor-licensing requirements. If site conditions, customer-requested changes, or the nature of the work place a project outside the exemption, AZHomeInstalls may decline, stop, or exclude that portion of the work.

Excluded work

AZHomeInstalls does not perform work requiring a contractor license or building permit. This includes work AZHomeInstalls has classified as outside its unlicensed service scope, such as hardwired electrical alterations, new circuits, panel work, regulated plumbing, gas, HVAC, structural alteration, roofing, and other work that requires licensure for the proposed scope.

Customer-provided products

If a customer supplies fixtures, hardware, mounts, furniture, devices, or other products, the customer is responsible for product selection, completeness, compatibility, and manufacturer defects. AZHomeInstalls is responsible for reasonable care in installation within the agreed scope but does not provide the manufacturer’s warranty.

Existing and concealed conditions

Pricing and scope are based on visible conditions and information supplied before work begins. Concealed damage, unsafe conditions, unsuitable mounting surfaces, missing structural support, or other conditions discovered after work begins may require work to stop or the scope to be revised.
